# Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act

## What this scheme gives you

A legal guarantee of at least 100 days of unskilled manual wage employment per
rural household in every financial year. This is not a discretionary welfare
payment: it is a statutory right, and the state has a duty to provide work
within 15 days of a valid demand, or to pay an unemployment allowance.

Wages are notified by the central government and revised from time to time,
and they differ by state. Wages must be paid into a bank or post office
account within 15 days of the work being completed; if payment is late, the
worker is entitled to compensation.

## Who is eligible

- Any rural household whose adult members are willing to do unskilled manual
  work. The household, not the individual, is the unit of entitlement, so the
  100 days are shared across the family.
- Every adult member of the household must be registered and hold a job card.
- There is no income ceiling, no caste restriction and no land requirement.

## Who is NOT eligible

Urban households are outside the Act. Work is unskilled manual labour, so
skilled work is not guaranteed under this scheme. Households that have already
exhausted their 100 days for the financial year cannot demand more work under
the Act, though some states provide extra days from their own funds.

## Documents you need

A job card, which is issued free of cost after registration, plus a bank or
post office account in the worker's name and Aadhaar. Photographs of adult
members are taken at registration.

## How to apply

Register at the Gram Panchayat with a written or oral request; the job card
must be issued within 15 days. To demand work, submit a written application
dated and receipted at the Panchayat, or apply on the NREGA portal. If work is
not provided within 15 days of a valid demand, you can claim the unemployment
allowance. Muster rolls and wage payments are public and can be checked on the
NREGA portal by job card number, which is how underpayment is usually caught.

## Common problems

Work offered beyond 5 km of the village entitles the worker to 10 per cent
extra wages and travel allowance. If a wage payment is short, the first step
is to check the muster roll entry and the bank credit on the portal, then raise
a complaint with the Block Programme Officer.
