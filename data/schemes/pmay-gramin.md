# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin

## What this scheme gives you

Financial assistance to build a pucca house with basic amenities for a rural
household that is homeless or living in a kutcha or dilapidated house. The
assistance is Rs 1.20 lakh in plain areas and Rs 1.30 lakh in hilly, difficult
and North-Eastern areas, released in instalments as construction progresses.

The house is also meant to get a toilet, electricity, an LPG connection and
piped water through convergence with other schemes, so the total value to the
family is higher than the cash assistance alone. Part of the assistance is
paid as wages for the family's own labour under MGNREGA.

## Who is eligible

- Households identified through the SECC 2011 housing deprivation data and
  then verified and included in the Awaas+ list.
- The beneficiary family must not already own a pucca house anywhere in India,
  and must not have received central assistance under any earlier housing
  scheme.
- The house must be built in the beneficiary's name, with a woman member
  either as sole owner or as co-owner.

## Who is NOT eligible

Urban households are covered by PMAY-Urban instead. Families that own a pucca
house, or that already took assistance under Indira Awaas Yojana or an earlier
PMAY-G sanction, are not eligible again. Government employees and
income-tax payers are normally excluded by the SECC-based selection.

## Documents you need

Aadhaar, job card or MGNREGA registration, bank account details, the Awaas+
registration number if already surveyed, and land documents for the plot. A
caste certificate is needed only for the category fields, not for eligibility.

## How to apply

The route is through the Gram Panchayat: ask whether the household appears in
the Awaas+ list, and if not, request inclusion during verification. The
Panchayat and the Block office conduct the survey and geo-tag the site. After
sanction, the first instalment is released on geo-tagged photographs of the
foundation, and later instalments follow the same inspection-linked pattern,
so construction must be photographed at each stage.

## What to watch out for

The instalments are tied to construction milestones and to geo-tagged photos,
which is the most common reason a payment is held up. Never pay a middleman
for inclusion in the list: the list is public on the PMAY-G portal and can be
checked by name, and the registration itself is free.
