# Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

## What this scheme gives you

Cashless hospital treatment worth up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for
secondary and tertiary care. The cover applies to hospitalisation, including
most surgery, medical treatment, day care procedures and pre-existing
diseases from day one. There is no cap on family size and no age limit: a
family of eight gets the same Rs 5 lakh as a family of two.

The money is paid to the hospital, not to you. Treatment is free at the point
of service at empanelled public and private hospitals, so there is no need to
arrange cash first and claim later.

## Who is eligible

- Families identified as deprived rural households under the SECC 2011
  deprivation criteria, and specified occupational categories of urban
  workers, such as street vendors, construction workers, domestic workers,
  sanitation workers and transport workers.
- All senior citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of income, under the
  separate Ayushman Vay Vandana card.
- Eligibility is checked against the family, not the individual, so one card
  covers everyone listed in the household.

## Who is NOT eligible

Families that are not in the SECC 2011 deprived list and are not in the
listed urban occupational categories are outside PM-JAY, and so are families
that already receive a comparable health cover from a state scheme that has
not been merged. Government employees covered by CGHS, ESIS or a state
medical reimbursement scheme normally use that scheme instead.

## Documents you need

Aadhaar or another government photo ID, a ration card or family document to
prove household membership, and a mobile number. The Ayushman card is
generated after e-KYC.

## How to apply

Check your name on the PM-JAY eligibility portal using your mobile number,
ration card number or Aadhaar. If the family appears in the list, complete
e-KYC at a Common Service Centre, an empanelled hospital's Ayushman desk, or
through the beneficiary app, and the card is issued. At the hospital, show the
Ayushman card at the help desk and the hospital obtains pre-authorisation
before treatment.

## What to watch out for

Ask at the hospital desk whether the specific procedure is in the PM-JAY
package list, because not every treatment is covered. Keep the discharge
summary and the pre-authorisation reference. If a hospital demands money for a
covered procedure, that is a grievance you can raise on the PM-JAY portal or
the national helpline 14555.
