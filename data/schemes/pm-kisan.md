# PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

## What this scheme gives you

An income support of Rs 6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of
Rs 2,000 each, directly into the bank account of the farmer family. The money
is unconditional: it is not a loan, it does not have to be repaid, and there is
no restriction on how you spend it.

## Who is eligible

- The applicant must be a landholding farmer family. The land records of the
  applicant's name must be in the village revenue records.
- Both husband and wife can be part of the same family claim, but the family
  as a unit is the beneficiary, not each individual separately.
- Aadhaar is required, and the Aadhaar number must be linked to an active bank
  account, because the instalment is transferred by Direct Benefit Transfer.
- e-KYC is mandatory and must be completed on the portal or at a Common
  Service Centre.

## Who is NOT eligible

The following categories are explicitly excluded, even if they own farmland:

- Institutional landholders, and farmer families where a member is a
  constitutional post holder or a serving or retired government employee
  (except Group D / Class IV employees).
- Families that paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers and chartered accountants
  who are registered with a professional body and are practising.
- Pensioners who receive a monthly pension of Rs 10,000 or more (except
  Class IV / Group D employees).

## Documents you need

Aadhaar card, land ownership papers (khatauni or equivalent record of rights),
a bank account in the applicant's own name, and a mobile number for OTP.

## How to apply

Self-registration is done on the PM-KISAN portal under the "New Farmer
Registration" section. After registration, the state land record officer
verifies the land details, and the district level officer approves the claim.
Status can be tracked on the portal using the registration number or Aadhaar.
If a payment fails, the usual causes are incomplete e-KYC, an Aadhaar that is
not seeded to the bank account, or a frozen or dormant account.

## Common reasons claims are rejected

Land record mismatch between the portal entry and the revenue record;
duplicate applications from the same family; an income-tax payer in the
family; and an account that is a joint account without the applicant as the
first holder.
