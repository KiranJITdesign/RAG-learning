# Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Caste students

## What this scheme gives you

Financial support for Scheduled Caste students studying after the matriculation
level, meaning class 11 and above, including diploma, undergraduate,
postgraduate and professional courses. The support has three parts: a
maintenance allowance paid monthly for the duration of the course, a
reimbursement of non-refundable fees charged by the institution, and a book
and study material grant.

The maintenance allowance is paid at a higher rate for hostellers than for day
scholars, and the amounts are revised from time to time.

## Who is eligible

- The student must belong to a Scheduled Caste, with a valid caste certificate
  in the prescribed format.
- The course must be a post-matriculation course recognised by the government,
  studied in a recognised institution.
- The annual family income from all sources must be within the prescribed
  ceiling, which is Rs 2.5 lakh per year for this scheme.
- The student must not be receiving any other scholarship or stipend for the
  same course from a government source.

## Who is NOT eligible

Students studying in classes up to the tenth standard are covered by
pre-matric schemes instead. Students who fail to be promoted to the next class
lose the scholarship for that year, and students who change course without
approval can have the award cancelled.

## Documents you need

Caste certificate, income certificate, Aadhaar, the previous year's mark sheet,
fee receipt from the institution, a bank account in the student's own name
linked to Aadhaar, and the institution's bonafide certificate.

## How to apply

Applications are made online on the National Scholarship Portal, usually
between the months announced each year, and the institution and the state
verify the application in sequence. The money is credited by direct benefit
transfer to the student's Aadhaar-linked account, and renewal applications
must be filed each year rather than being automatic.

## What to watch out for

The single most common reason for rejection is a name or date-of-birth
mismatch between Aadhaar, the caste certificate and the institution's records,
so check all three before submitting. Keep the application number and the fee
receipt, because fee reimbursement is processed only after the institution
confirms the admission.
