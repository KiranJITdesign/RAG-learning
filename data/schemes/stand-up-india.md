# Stand-Up India

## What this scheme gives you

A bank loan between Rs 10 lakh and Rs 1 crore to set up a new enterprise in
manufacturing, trading or the services sector. Banks are expected to finance
up to 75 per cent of the project cost, with the borrower bringing the
remaining margin, and the loan is covered by a credit guarantee so that
collateral is normally not required.

Every bank branch in the country is expected to fund at least one such
enterprise by a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe borrower and one by a woman
entrepreneur, which is why the scheme is branch-driven rather than
portal-driven.

## Who is eligible

- A borrower who is a Scheduled Caste, Scheduled Tribe or woman entrepreneur,
  and who is at least 18 years of age.
- The enterprise must be greenfield, meaning a first-time venture in
  manufacturing, services or the trading sector, and must not be a takeover of
  an existing business.
- For non-individual applicants, at least 51 per cent of the shareholding must
  be held by a Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe person, or by a woman
  entrepreneur.

## Who is NOT eligible

A business that already exists is not greenfield and cannot be funded under
this scheme. Borrowers who are already in default with a bank, and allied
agricultural activities, are outside the scope, since those have separate
schemes.

## Documents you need

Caste certificate if applying in the SC or ST category, a detailed project
report, proof of identity and address, the firm's registration or Udyam
certificate, quotations for machinery or equipment, and the last few years of
financials if the applicant is a company.

## How to apply

Apply on the Stand-Up Mitra portal, or approach the nearest Scheduled
Commercial Bank, Regional Rural Bank or Small Finance Bank branch directly. A
handholding support agency is available for pre-loan training and for help
preparing the project report, and this service is free for eligible
borrowers. Sanction depends on the bank's credit assessment of the project
report, so a realistic cash-flow projection matters more than the scheme
itself.

## What to watch out for

The loan is a commercial loan and has to be repaid, so treat the 75 per cent
financing as leverage rather than a subsidy. Ask the branch for the credit
guarantee cover and the effective interest rate in writing before you sign.
