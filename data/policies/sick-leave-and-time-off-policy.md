# Sick Leave, Casual Leave & Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Sick Leave Entitlement

Every full-time employee is entitled to **12 days of fully paid sick and medical leave** per calendar year (credited at 1.0 day per month of service).
- **Short-term illness (1-2 consecutive days)**: No doctor's note or medical certificate is required. Simply notify your direct manager and log the absence in the HR portal under "Sick Leave".
- **Extended illness (3 or more consecutive days)**: A signed medical certificate or prescription from a licensed healthcare practitioner must be uploaded to the HR portal upon return.
- **Mental Health Days**: Employees can use up to 3 days of sick leave per year as mental health recovery days with no medical justification required.
- **Rollover**: Up to 6 unused sick leave days roll over into the following year. Any remaining balance beyond 6 days lapses and cannot be encashed.

## Annual Paid Time Off (PTO) & Casual Leave

- Full-time employees accrue **18 days of paid vacation/casual leave** annually (accrued at 1.5 days per month).
- Advance notice of at least 5 business days is requested for vacations lasting 3 days or longer.
- Emergency casual leave can be taken same-day by pinging the `#team-out-of-office` Slack channel.

## How to Apply & Report Leave

1. **Log in to the HR Portal**: Visit `https://workday.internal.corp` using your Single Sign-On credentials.
2. **Navigate to Time Off**: Select "Time Off & Leave Requests" -> "Request Absence".
3. **Select Leave Type**: Choose "Medical / Sick Leave" or "Annual PTO".
4. **Dates & Note**: Pick the start and end dates. Add a brief optional note for coverage handoff.
5. **Manager Notification**: The system automatically emails your manager and marks your Slack status as "Out of Office (Sick Leave)" with calendar protection.
6. **Support Assistance**: If you are bedridden or unable to access the portal, message your team lead or ask the Internal Ops Co-Pilot to draft a notification to HR on your behalf.
