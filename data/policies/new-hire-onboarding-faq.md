# New Hire Onboarding: First 30 Days Playbook

## Week 1: IT Setup & Core Accounts

1. **Day 1 Orientation**: Attend the virtual welcome session at 10:00 AM local time with HR People Ops.
2. **Account Access**: Verify login to your email, Slack, GitHub, Jira, and Confluence via Okta SSO.
3. **Hardware Check**: Confirm your laptop, monitor, and peripherals arrived in good order. Report any missing item to `#it-helpdesk`.
4. **Slack Channels to Join**:
   - `#welcome-new-hires` — Introduce yourself to the company!
   - `#announcements` — Company-wide updates.
   - `#it-helpdesk` — Immediate IT questions and hardware tickets.
   - `#team-[your-dept]` — Your functional squad channel.

## Week 2: Benefits Enrollment & 1-on-1s

- **Health Insurance**: Complete medical, dental, and vision insurance enrollment in the benefits portal within your first 30 days. Coverage begins on your first calendar day.
- **Direct Deposit / Payroll**: Submit your bank routing details and tax documentation in the HR Workday portal.
- **Onboarding Buddy**: You have been paired with an onboarding buddy from your department. Schedule a 30-minute sync to review team rituals and deployment workflows.

## Need Help Anytime?
Use the **Internal Ops Co-Pilot** to ask any question regarding HR benefits, IT tickets, software licenses, or company policies. If an issue requires human intervention, the Co-Pilot can automatically draft and submit tickets directly to HR or IT.
