# Hardware, Peripherals and IT Equipment Catalog

## Standard Developer & Staff Hardware Bundle

All newly hired employees receive a company-provisioned workstation delivered prior to Day 1:
- **Engineering / Data Roles**: Apple MacBook Pro 14" or 16" (M3 Pro/Max, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD) or Lenovo ThinkPad P1 Gen 6 (Intel i7, 32GB RAM, Ubuntu/Windows dual-boot).
- **Product, Design & Operations**: Apple MacBook Pro 14" (M3, 18GB RAM) or Dell XPS 15.
- **External Display**: One Dell UltraSharp 27" 4K USB-C Hub Monitor (U2723QE) supplied for remote/hybrid employees.

## Peripherals & Home Office Stipend

Each employee is allotted a **$300 (or INR 25,000) one-time peripheral stipend** in their first month to order:
- Ergonomic keyboard (e.g. Logitech MX Keys, Apple Magic Keyboard)
- Ergonomic wireless mouse (e.g. Logitech MX Master 3S)
- Noise-canceling USB headset or webcam (Logitech Brio 4K / Jabra Evolve)
- Laptop riser stand and surge protector

You can purchase these through the Corporate Amazon Business punchout catalog or expense them via Expensify with receipts under "New Hire Hardware Setup".

## Defective Equipment & Refresh Cycle

- **Hardware Refreshes**: Laptops are replaced every 3 years.
- **Broken / Malfunctioning Equipment**: If your charger, screen, keyboard, or battery malfunctions, do not attempt unauthorized local repairs. Contact IT via the Internal Ops Co-Pilot or submit a hardware replacement ticket for next-business-day swap.
